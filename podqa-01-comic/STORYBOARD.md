# STORYBOARD — PodQAst Inteligente #01, as a comic

Extract of `episodes/podqa-01.mp3`: **0:39.40 → 1:23.97** (44.57 s), block B of the episode.
1920×1080, 24 fps, 1080p. Three pages, nine panels, one panel per beat. The cut is hard: a new panel
is a new place to look, so there is no dissolve between them — only the page around the frame, which
never moves, tells you that the panels belong to one sheet.

**Logline of the extract.** A spacecraft is lost over a unit of measurement, two teams each did the
arithmetic correctly, and the reason is not the arithmetic.

## Lettering

Everything on screen is the episode's own Portuguese: short phrases only, never a full sentence, because
a balloon is a beat and not a subtitle. Nine balloons, three captions, five sound effects.

`PX(panel, n)` converts screen pixels to page units per panel, so the type is the same size on screen in
a 1760-wide strip and in a 1000-wide panel.

## What is deliberately not here

No captions with numbers on screen, no subtitles of the whole sentence, no gradient anywhere, no glow,
no blur. Shadows are a halftone plate offset by 16 page units. If a device needs a gradient to work, it
does not belong on this page.
