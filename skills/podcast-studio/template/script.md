## Cold open

HOST: Here is a question worth an episode, asked as plainly as I can: why does the thing everyone agrees on keep not happening?

GUEST: Because agreeing is cheap. Paying for it is not.

HOST: That is the episode. Let's find out what "paying for it" actually costs.

[beat 1.5]

## Chapter one — the part nobody budgets for

GUEST: The invisible cost is attention, and it is spent before anyone writes a line of code.

HOST: Give me a concrete case. Something small enough to see all the way through.

GUEST: A team spends six weeks building the thing nobody asked for, and one afternoon discovering it. The afternoon was the cheap part.

[beat 1.0]

HOST: So the win is not building faster, it is finding out sooner.

GUEST: And finding out sooner means shipping something embarrassing on purpose, on a schedule.

## Chapter two — what survives

HOST: If we accept that, what should the team actually protect?

GUEST: The feedback loop, and the willingness to be wrong early. Everything else is negotiable.

HOST: Last question. What would you tell someone starting this tomorrow?

GUEST: Pick the smallest honest version, put it in front of someone, and let the silence tell you where to look.
