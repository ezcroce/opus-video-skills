## Promise points

1. A bug that costs millions is usually not an arithmetic error — it is a missing contract between two teams, and one question finds it.
2. You can use a chat model this week to list the edge cases that break a function, as long as you review the list instead of accepting it.

## Tone

Conversational interview podcast, in the spirit of *Mamilos* (Ju Wallauer and Cris Bartheis) that the original prompt points at: direct, no announcer voice, one question to the listener in the first sentence, zero jargon without a gloss.

## Must mention

- The Mars Climate Orbiter, told as a QA story rather than a space story.
- That the AI tool does not know the business rule, and will invent requirements confidently.

## Must avoid

- Jargon without a gloss.
- Bare numbers and abbreviations the voice would have to guess (`1999`, `QA`, `IA`).
- More than five minutes.
