# STORYBOARD — "A FORMIGUEIRO" (the anthill)

A 23 s vertical pixel short. Same engine and look as the reference reel, different story and a
cooler palette: the reference's ant wants to *become* a queen; this one is a young ant who wants to
*leave* the anthill, and the world pushes back.

Everything is drawn in code; nothing is imported, sampled or generated by a model.

## Logline

A young ant in the warm dark of the anthill is drawn up by a lamp she cannot reach, and when she
finally climbs into the light she finds the sky full of tall green grass and a world of giants.

## World and palette

The same house palette as the reference, shifted cooler and greener to separate it from the
source reel:

| Ramp | Values | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Soil | `#3a1d14` → `#2a1410` → `#1c0c08` | the anthill — cooler and darker than the reference's brown |
| Sky | `#7fa8b8` → `#4d7f92` → `#2d5666` | a cold blue daylight, the opposite of the reference's orange |
| Grass | `#2f5c34` → `#6aa845` → `#a8d46a` | the tall stalks, brighter and cooler |
| Amber | `#f0a83c` | the lamp, the embers, the amber half of every caption |
| Violet | `#b088e8` | the mushrooms, which stay the one warm-violet accent |
| Cream | `#e8e0d0` | the cream half of every caption, the ant's mandibles |

The amber lamp and the violet mushrooms are carried over unchanged on purpose: they are the
style's signature, and the cool soil and sky make them glow harder.

## The cast

- **The ant** — the same three-segment body, six legs, cream mandibles, dark red-brown. Small.
- **The lamp** — a chain from off the top of frame, a dark dome, amber flame, violet licks.
- **The mushrooms** — violet domes with a glow underneath.
- **The stalks** — tall, thin, brighter and cooler than the reference's.

## Motif

**The ladder.** The ant is always climbing toward something: up out of the dark, up the chain,
up into the grass. Each shot moves her up in the frame. The last shot puts her at the surface
with the whole sky above her, which is the first time she is not the smallest thing on screen.

## The arc

Curiosity → effort → doubt → arrival. She leaves because she wants to; the world does not make it
easy; she arrives anyway.

## The captions

Two lines per shot, cream and amber word by word, in the lower band, 24 px apart at scale 2. The
first line arrives 0.1 s into the shot, the second 1.2 s later; each fades out before the next
shot's arrive.

## What to check when reviewing

- The pixel grid is visible at 1080 wide.
- The sky is dithered, not a smooth gradient.
- The lamp's light falls downward and fades to nothing at its edge.
- The captions are legible, in the lower band, and gone before the next shot.
- The ant is the smallest thing in shot 2 and the largest in shot 5.
