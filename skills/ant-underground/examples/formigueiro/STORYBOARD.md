# STORYBOARD — "A FORMIGUEIRO" (the worked example)

A 23 s vertical pixel short in the same four-beat structure as the reference, on a cooler palette,
about a different colony.

## The captions come first

```
ESTE FORMIGUEIRO
FORMIGAS

EL QUER
TRABALHAR

TRABALHA AQUI
QUEM COMENTA

DEBAIXO
ELA CAVOU
```

Read as a sentence: *this is an anthill. Ants. He wants to work. Work here. Whoever comments.
Below, she dug.*

Note what the second half does: the reference's invitation was **"quem segue"** (follow), this one
is **"quem comenta"** (comment) — the same grammatical move, a different button. The comment is
framed as **being given a job**, which is what the reference does with *"quem comenta, que
trabalho terá"*. The reward is a role in the world, not a prize.

## The four beats

| # | Time | Beat | Captions | The picture | The picture's job |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–4.0 | **The claim** | `ESTE FORMIGUEIRO` / `FORMIGAS` | the title card: a cold blue sky in the top quarter, the anthill below, the golden emblem between the two lines | the claim needs a *place* — so the frame is split between above and below, and the emblem is the seal of the place |
| 2 | 4.0–9.0 | **The want** | `EL QUER` / `TRABALHAR` | the lamp, the ant alone under it in the pool of light, two violet mushrooms in the dark | the want is *solitude under a light you cannot reach* — and the caption is the colony's job, not a personal dream |
| 3 | 9.0–14.0 | **The invitation** | `TRABALHA AQUI` / `QUEM COMENTA` | the lamp bigger and lower, the ant under it, the light overhead | the subject drops onto the viewer: *you* work here. The light is overhead because the viewer arrives from above |
| 4 | 14.0–19.0 | **The tease** | `DEBAIXO` / `ELA CAVOU` | the surface: cold blue sky, tall green stalks, the ant digging with a pale sickle | the story goes deeper, so we cut away from the light — and end on a hole, not a resolution |
| 5 | 19.0–23.0 | **The closer** | `ESTE FORMIGUEIRO` / `FORMIGAS` | the title card again, the emblem shrinking away, the frame fading to black | re-state the world the viewer has been let into, and leave the hole open |

## The grammar, checked

- `EL QUER` — first person, the speaker's want. ✓
- `TRABALHA AQUI` — no subject; the viewer supplies it. ✓
- `QUEM COMENTA` — the condition named explicitly, the CTA inside the fiction. ✓
- `DEBAIXO` — the direction of the tease: *deeper*. ✓
- `ELA CAVOU` — the subject changes; *she* dug. ✓

The subject keeps moving between the speaker and the viewer. That movement is the story.

## What changes against the reference

- **The palette is cool.** A blue sky over a cool brown anthill instead of orange over warm brown.
  The amber lamp and the violet mushrooms are carried over **unchanged**, on purpose: they are the
  style's signature, and against a cooler world they glow harder.
- **The invitation targets a different button** — comments, not follows.
- **The motive is work, not becoming.** The reference's ant wants to *be* an ant; this one wants
  to *work*, and the colony needs workers. That is why the comment is the reward: a commenter
  gets a job.
- **The climb.** The ant rises through the frame between shots 3 and 4 — the ladder motif — and
  the drone's pitch rises with her (A1 → B1 → C2).

## The visual grammar

- **The light is the invitation.** The lamp is the only warm thing in a dark world, always overhead.
- **Cut to the sky to leave the colony.**
- **The emblem is the seal.** Golden ring, on the claim and the closer, nowhere else.

## What to check when reviewing

- The pixel grid is visible at 1080 wide.
- The sky is dithered, not a smooth gradient.
- The lamp's light falls downward and fades to nothing at its edge.
- The captions are legible, in the lower band, and gone before the next shot.
- Shot 3's lamp is bigger and lower than shot 2's — the light overhead *is* the invitation.
- The reel ends on the hole, not on a resolution.
