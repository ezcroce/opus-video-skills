# STORYBOARD — the reference reel

The captions come first. This is the whole reel in five lines:

```
ESTE FORMIGUEIRO
FORMIGAS

QUER SER
VIRA FORMIGA

VIRA FORMIGA
QUEM SEGUE

DO GÉU
ELA CAVOU
```

Read as a sentence: *this is an anthill. Ants. I want to become. [You] become an ant. Whoever
follows, from the sky. From the sky, she dug.*

The subject of "vira formiga" is dropped on purpose — and it is **the viewer**, not the speaker.
That is the entire mechanism. See [../../references/storytelling.md](../../references/storytelling.md).

## Structure

| # | Time | Beat | Captions | The picture | The picture's job |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–4.0 | **The claim** | `ESTE FORMIGUEIRO` / `FORMIGAS` | the title card: sky in the top quarter, the anthill below, the golden ring emblem between the two lines | the claim needs a *place* — so the frame is split between above and below, and the emblem is the seal of the place |
| 2 | 4.0–9.0 | **The want** | `QUER SER` / `VIRA FORMIGA` | the lamp, the ant alone under it in the pool of light, two violet mushrooms in the dark | the want is *solitude under a light you cannot reach* |
| 3 | 9.0–14.0 | **The invitation** | `VIRA FORMIGA` / `QUEM SEGUE` | the lamp bigger and lower, the ant under it, the light overhead | the subject drops onto the viewer; the light is overhead because *quem segue* means arriving from above |
| 4 | 14.0–19.0 | **The tease** | `DO GÉU` / `ELA CAVOU` | the surface: orange sky, tall green stalks, the ant digging with a pale sickle | the story leaves the colony, so we cut to the sky; it ends on a hole, not a resolution |
| 5 | 19.0–23.0 | **The closer** | `ESTE FORMIGUEIRO` / `FORMIGAS` | the title card again, the emblem shrinking away, the frame fading to black | re-state the world the viewer has been let into, and leave the hole open |

## Why the captions are two lines

Line 1 sets it up, line 2 lands it about 1.2 s later. The reference never uses a single line
alone except in the tease, where the second line does the work of a full stop.

Every line is **at most two words**. A reel plays muted more often than not, and a caption is
read at a glance in a feed.

## The grammar, checked

- `QUER SER` — first person, the speaker's want. ✓
- `VIRA FORMIGA` — no subject. The viewer supplies it. ✓
- `QUEM SEGUE` — the condition named explicitly ("whoever follows"), the CTA inside the fiction. ✓
- `DO GÉU` — picks up "quem segue": the viewer comes from the sky. ✓
- `ELA CAVOU` — the subject changes again; *she* dug. Another ant, deeper down. ✓

The subject keeps moving between the speaker and the viewer. That movement is the story.

## The visual grammar

- **The light is the invitation.** The lamp is the only warm thing in a dark world, and it is
  always overhead.
- **Cut to the sky to leave the colony.** Orange sky, green stalks, a hard horizontal ground line.
- **The emblem is the seal.** Golden ring, on the claim and the closer, nowhere else.

## What to check when reviewing

- The pixel grid is visible at 1080 wide.
- The sky is dithered, not a smooth gradient.
- The lamp's light falls downward and fades to nothing at its edge.
- The captions are legible, in the lower band, and gone before the next shot.
- Each picture does the job the caption gave it — especially shot 3, where the lamp must be
  bigger and lower than in shot 2, because the light overhead *is* "from the sky".
- The reel ends on the hole, not on a resolution.
