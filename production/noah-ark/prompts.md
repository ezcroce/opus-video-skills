# Noè e l'Arca — shot prompts for Kling 3.0

Paste the **Style line** and **Character bible** into every shot that needs them. Attach the Noè and family element images. Dialogue and narration are in Italian in quotation marks. Each shot is ≤15 s.

## Style line (shared)

Cinematic 3D cartoon animation, Pixar-quality character design, expressive faces, warm biblical landscapes, dramatic volumetric lighting, detailed environments, family-friendly, 16:9.

## Character bible (shared)

- **Noè:** elderly man, long white beard, grey hair, kind weathered face, cream linen tunic, brown mantle. Same design in every shot.
- **Noè's wife:** older woman, grey hair in a cream head-cloth, blue-grey robe.
- **Sons (Shem, Ham, Japheth):** young men, short dark hair, earth-toned tunics, sleeves rolled up for work.
- **Ark:** huge wooden hull, dark gopher-wood planks, three decks, a ramp at the side.

Constraints tail (add to every shot): stable character design, consistent face and beard, no morphing, no extra limbs, stable horizon, steady temporal consistency.

Use one narrator voice across all scenes (deep, warm, male). Lines in order:

1. Scene 1: "Molto tempo fa, la Terra era diventata un luogo pieno di violenza e cattiveria. Gli uomini avevano dimenticato Dio e i suoi insegnamenti. Ma in mezzo a quel mondo, c'era un uomo diverso: Noè."

7. Scene 8: "Per quaranta giorni e quaranta notti la pioggia cadde sulla Terra. Le acque crebbero e l'arca galleggiò sopra ogni cosa."
